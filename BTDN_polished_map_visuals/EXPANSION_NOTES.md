# Bloons TD N — Tower Expansion

This update adds the **8 towers that were missing** from the Bloons TD 6 roster. Each one has 3 upgrade paths x 5 tiers (**120 upgrades**, all with real gameplay effects), original Canvas-drawn artwork, and works on all 3 maps x 3 difficulties.

## What was already in the game (not duplicated)
Dart, Boomerang, Bomb, Tack, Ice, Glue, Sniper, Heli, Ninja, Wizard, Super, Alchemist, Banana Farm, Monkey Village (14 towers). They are untouched and keep their single upgrade.

## What was added
| Tower | Category | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dartling Gunner | Military | $800 | Rapid-fire gatling. Can be aimed at your cursor. |
| Mortar Monkey | Military | $750 | Lobs shells onto bloons anywhere in its huge range. |
| Monkey Sub | Military | $325 | Water only. Darts, missiles and a nuclear reactor. **Water only.** |
| Monkey Buccaneer | Military | $500 | Water only. Broadsides, aircraft carrier and a trade empire. **Water only.** |
| Monkey Ace | Military | $800 | Flies endless loops over its airstrip, strafing and bombing. |
| Druid | Magic | $400 | Nature magic: thorns, lightning storms and a healing forest. |
| Spike Factory | Support | $1000 | Lays spike piles on the track that pop bloons. |
| Engineer Monkey | Support | $400 | Nail gun, deployable sentries, overclock and foam. |

## Upgrade rules
BTD6 crosspathing: at most **two** paths can be upgraded, and only **one** of them may go past tier 2 (so 5-2-0, 2-5-0, 0-2-5 ... are legal; 3-3-0 is not). The panel greys out and explains locked upgrades.

## Gameplay features worth knowing
- **Naval towers (Sub, Buccaneer)** can only be placed on water. *End of Road* uses its pool; the other maps provide appropriate water zones so naval towers have a home. Land towers cannot be placed on those ponds. While a naval tower is selected, the water pulses on the map.
- **Targeting** (First / Last / Close / Strong) is available for every new tower that picks targets. **Dartling Gunner** also has *Aim: Cursor* to fire where your mouse points.
- **Support effects are real:** Monkey Sub's Support path buffs nearby towers (the same attack-speed buff mechanism Monkey Village uses, so it works on the original towers too); Engineer *Overclock* speeds up other towers; Merchantman / Jungle's Bounty pay money at the end of each round; Spirit of the Forest heals lives (never above the starting lives).
- **Slows, stuns and freezes do not affect blimps** (MOAB-class) for the new towers.
- **Mobile:** the page now has a viewport tag, the canvas scales to the screen, taps map to the right game position, and the upgrade panel becomes a bottom sheet with a close button.
- Unaffordable towers are dimmed in the menu; new towers have a gold outline.

## Things that are NOT implemented (and why)
- **Paragons:** not added. They require three tier-5 towers of one type plus pop-count tracking, and the 14 original towers have only a single upgrade, so the prerequisites cannot be met correctly.
- **The 14 original towers keep their single upgrade each.** They were not converted to 3 x 5 paths, to avoid rewriting working code. This is the obvious next step if you want it.
- **Camo / lead / purple / etc. do not exist in this game's bloon system**, so upgrades about camo detection or lead popping were replaced with effects that do work here (pierce, ceramic and blimp damage, burning, slowing, stunning, economy, healing, support).
- Prices, stats and names are *inspired by* Bloons TD 6 and rebalanced for this game's economy; they are not exact copies. All artwork is original Canvas drawing; **no official Bloons artwork is used**.
- The game has no save system, so none was added.
- Left as-is on purpose: the existing 4px click offset on desktop and the settings menu not pausing the game.

## Publishing to GitHub Pages
1. Copy `btd_expansion.js` and the 9 updated `.html` files into the root of your `noam_BTDN` repository (same folder as the other game files; overwrite the old HTML files).
2. `git add . && git commit -m "Add 8 new towers" && git push`
3. In the repo: Settings > Pages, make sure the source is your main branch (it already is if the site works today). Wait a minute, then hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5) the site.
4. Note: your menu page is named `indax.html` (not `index.html`). If the live site root loads a different `index.html`, leave that file alone; it needs no change.

## How it was tested
Headless Chromium against all 9 files: zero console errors; every new tower placed with real mouse clicks; all 120 upgrades verified unique and stat-changing; crosspath locking; late-game (round 58) runs of every maxed path; income, healing, buff aura, overclock, spikes, mines, sentries, water placement and selling; regression of all 14 original towers; x3 fast-forward; cursor aim; stress test of ~48 towers vs ~390 bloons (~0.85 ms per tick); phone-size touch flow.
